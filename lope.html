Tension

We aim to create a community where users can communicate

Idea

Right now, an app like this gets used once — to pre-order the album — then forgotten. The goal: give fans a reason to keep opening it, with a space to talk about the band built into the app itself.

Execution

The design stays minimal — a page built around very few elements, with the album cover taking over as a full background whenever possible, keeping the band's visual identity front and center.
