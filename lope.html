How do you keep fans opening an app after the pre-order?

ClientSelf-initiated — Google UX Design Certificate
ScopeApp concept, user research, UI, prototype

01 — Idea

Right now, an app like this gets used once — to pre-order the album — then forgotten. The goal: give fans a reason to keep opening it, with a space to talk about the band built into the app itself. Research was based on interviews: who the users are, and what would bring them back after the first purchase.

02 — Execution

The usability test changed the layout. Repeating the same button on every page annoyed most testers, so it moved into the app bar — breaking the symmetry to pull attention to the action. The rest stays minimal: very few elements, the album cover as a full background whenever possible.

Usability findings
01Most participants needed help finding the product — the page organisation got in the way.
02Most wanted to recheck their details before confirming the purchase.
03They wanted a full recap of their order at checkout.

03 — Outcome

Case study with an interactive Figma prototype.
