Blindex makes custom roller blinds for offices, hotels, and commercial spaces — moving smoothly, quietly, always controlled. That gesture became the foundation for the logo, the tone, the palette: Dynamic Flow. The "e" carries the concept, its shape echoing the scrolling motion of a blind. Deep Black and Ardesia Red anchor the palette; Comfortaa sets the tone for titles, Source Sans Pro keeps the rest clean and unobtrusive.
