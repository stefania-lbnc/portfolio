Giving an institution with decades of history a look that feels current.

Idea

The magazine promotes tourism through Egypt's ancient sites and living culture, alongside a look back at the Academy's exhibitions and events from the year before.

Execution

The redesign moved toward something cleaner and more contemporary: a simplified grid, more breathing room on the page, and a typographic hierarchy built to guide the eye rather than crowd it.

Issue after issue, it reads considered rather than assembled.
