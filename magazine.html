A.D.E. Magazine

How do you make an institution with decades of history look current?

ClientEgyptian Academy in Rome
ScopeMagazine redesign, layout

03 — Outcome

Redesign carried through four+ published issues.

ClientEgyptian Academy in Rome
ScopeMagazine redesign, layout

01 — Idea

The magazine promotes tourism through Egypt's ancient sites and living culture, alongside a look back at the Academy's exhibitions and events from the year before.

02 — Execution

The redesign moved toward something cleaner and more contemporary: a simplified grid, more breathing room on the page, and a typographic hierarchy built to guide the eye rather than crowd it. Issue after issue, it reads considered rather than assembled.

03 — Outcome

Redesign carried through four+ published issues.
