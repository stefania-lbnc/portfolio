05 / 07 — Brand identity · 2024

Terraviva

No leaf, no obvious eco-icon. Then what?

ClientSelf-initiated — contest entry, with Leandro Marin
ScopeLogo, visual identity

01 — Idea

The concept starts from TerraViva's own philosophy instead: innovative practices built around environmental and social sustainability. Crafted from golden triangles, the butterfly carries that idea directly — transformation, and the balance between nature and geometry. A modern, urban read on what sustainability can look like.

02 — Execution

Typeface sets the tone before a single word is read. Comfortaa Bold gives it structure; Poppins keeps it grounded. Co-created with Leandro Marin.
